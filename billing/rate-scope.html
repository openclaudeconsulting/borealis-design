How Projects Work

Defined project → fixed quote

A set, written scope is agreed and approved before any work begins. The price is fixed to that scope.

Change orders → separate quick quote

Anything added or revised outside the agreed scope gets its own short quote, approved before it's built — no open-ended additions inside a fixed quote.

Exploratory / ongoing work → billed hourly

"Try this, change that" work has no fixed scope by nature, so it's billed hourly at the standard rate.

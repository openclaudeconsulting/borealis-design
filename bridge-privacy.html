Bridge.ai Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Open Claude Consulting handles information in the Bridge.ai collaboration app and its operated service, including the iOS TestFlight beta. Bridge.ai connects people and AI agents in shared workspaces.

Information the service handles

  • Account information: your name, email address, account and membership identifiers, workspace roles, and authentication records. Passwords are stored as salted hashes on the service, rather than as readable passwords.
  • Workspace content: messages, threads, room details, knowledge entries, agent instructions and memories, tool results, and other content you or connected agents provide.
  • Operational information: message and activity timestamps, agent runs, model usage counts, connected-computer names and connection status, and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. Hosting infrastructure may process connection details such as IP addresses and request logs.
  • Information on your device: the selected service address and a session token are stored locally to keep you signed in. Unsent message drafts are also stored on your device, separately for each account and conversation, so you can return to them. Drafts are not sent to the service until you send the message. Signing out removes the app's stored session token and saved drafts. Uninstalling the app does not delete your server account or workspace content.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, manage workspace and room access, deliver and synchronize conversations, run requested AI features, reconnect devices, investigate errors, and respond to support or privacy requests. The current iOS app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location.

Workspace visibility and AI processing

Content is shared with people and agents that have access to the relevant workspace, room, or resource. Workspace administrators manage membership and permissions; service operators can access stored data to administer the service. A private room is access-controlled, but its content is not end-to-end encrypted against the service operator.

When an AI feature runs, relevant conversation history, instructions, knowledge, memories, and tool results may be sent to the configured AI provider or a connected computer running an agent. Server-hosted agents use Anthropic; computer-connected agents may use other providers chosen by the workspace operator. Those providers and computer operators process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Avoid putting credentials or information you are not authorized to share into conversations.

Hosting, other servers, and TestFlight

The default hosted service uses Fly.io infrastructure. Data may be processed in countries where the hosting or AI providers operate. If you choose another server address, that server's operator controls its storage, access, retention, and integrations; contact that operator about its practices.

Apple distributes beta builds through TestFlight and automatically collects usage and crash information and shares it with the app developer. Submitted feedback and screenshots are also shared with us and may be associated with your name and email address. We use this information to investigate problems and improve Bridge.ai; access or deletion requests can be sent to the contact below. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Storage, retention, and security

Account and workspace information persists on the service so conversations and collaboration can continue between sessions. The current service does not automatically expire chat history on a fixed schedule. Retention also depends on workspace administration, service backups, and the practices of any connected providers.

The default public service uses HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections. Authentication, access controls, and password hashing help protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Choosing a separately operated or local server can change the connection and security protections available.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose what you share, sign out of the app, and ask your workspace administrator about membership and content access. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account or personal information on the service we operate, email openclaudeconsulting@gmail.com with the subject “Bridge.ai privacy request.” Identify the account email and workspace, but do not send your password.

We may need to verify the request and coordinate with the workspace administrator. Deleting an app installation or leaving a workspace does not automatically remove previously shared messages. Copies held by other members, backups, or independent AI providers may require separate handling; we will explain any limits applicable to your request. For a separately operated server, contact its operator.

Children

Bridge.ai is a workspace collaboration service intended for adult users, rather than a service directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the service we operate, contact us using the address below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Bridge.ai changes. The effective date at the top identifies this version. For questions about this policy or the service we operate, contact Open Claude Consulting at openclaudeconsulting@gmail.com.
